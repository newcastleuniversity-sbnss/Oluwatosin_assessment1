CC   -!- FUNCTION: Monomeric heme protein which primary function is to store
CC       oxygen and facilitate its diffusion within muscle tissues. Reversibly
CC       binds oxygen through a pentacoordinated heme iron and enables its
CC       timely and efficient release as needed during periods of heightened
CC       demand (PubMed:30918256, PubMed:34679218). Depending on the oxidative
CC       conditions of tissues and cells, and in addition to its ability to bind
CC       oxygen, it also has a nitrite reductase activity whereby it regulates
CC       the production of bioactive nitric oxide (PubMed:32891753). Under
CC       stress conditions, like hypoxia and anoxia, it also protects cells
CC       against reactive oxygen species thanks to its pseudoperoxidase activity
CC       (PubMed:34679218). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:30918256,
CC       ECO:0000269|PubMed:32891753, ECO:0000269|PubMed:34679218}.
